# Ideas

Possibilities to discuss and explore. These are not implementation tasks.

## How to use

- Read this file only when explicitly asked to add, discuss, review or organise ideas.
- Group ideas under **Campaign**, **Whole game** or **After the campaign**. Pick the main scope; mention any overlap in the description.
- Give each idea the next unused `IDEA-NNN` ID. Keep that ID when moving or updating the idea.
- Record the date (Vienna local date), author, scope and status. Status is **Exploring**, **Accepted** or **Shelved**.
- Keep the description short: what might happen, why it would help, and open questions. Unresolved details are welcome.
- Do not implement ideas or move them into a backlog automatically. When Pierre decides to turn one into work, add a concrete item to [backlog.md](backlog.md) using its format, link back to the idea, and mark the idea **Accepted** with a link to that backlog item.
- Keep agreed campaign narrative in [GameStory.md](../../GameStory.md).

Template:

```markdown
### IDEA-NNN — Title

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Author: Name
Scope: Campaign | Whole game | After the campaign
Status: Exploring

Short description of the idea.

Why:
What it would improve.

Open questions:
- What still needs discussion?
```

## Campaign

### IDEA-003 — Wisps incarnate NPCs for role-play

Date: 2026-11-07
Author: Pierre
Scope: Campaign
Status: Exploring

A player in wisp form could incarnate an NPC, take control of that character, and role-play them with the other players.

Why:
Give wisps an active role in scenes and make NPC encounters more lively through player improvisation.

Open questions:
- Which NPCs can wisps control, and how do players choose or release one?
- What guidance does the player receive about the NPC's personality and role in the scene?
- How does role-play fit with the NPC's scripted actions and story progression?

### IDEA-004 — An earlier campaign role for Sol, Anna and Konstantin

Date: 2026-11-07
Author: Pierre
Scope: Campaign
Status: Exploring

Sol, Anna and Konstantin enter the story in the Adult chapter. Their players should have a way to take part in the campaign before then, beyond joining minigames.

Two options to explore:

- Add a short visit to the Adult chapter before Young Adult, introducing and unlocking these three characters early.
- Make the cats Silex and Taiga available to incarnate from the start, giving these players temporary roles until their own characters enter the story. This provides only two roles for three people, so a third role or another arrangement would still be needed.

Why:
Let all three players participate in exploration and story interactions earlier in the evening.

Open questions:
- How would an early visit to Adult fit into the campaign's narrative and progression?
- What meaningful actions could the cats perform in the campaign?
- If using temporary roles, how do all three players participate, and when do they switch to their own characters?

## Whole game

### IDEA-002 — Player interactions trigger group animations

Date: 2026-11-07
Author: Pierre
Scope: Whole game
Status: Exploring

Player interactions could trigger coordinated avatar animations: group hugs, players turning in a circle, “C’est la chenille qui redémarre”, or a “gang bang” (choreography to define). This extends the effects discussed in the [character interactions proposal](proposal-character-interactions.md) to group animations.

Why:
Give players playful ways to create shared moments during the evening.

Open questions:
- Which interactions trigger each animation, and how do other players join?
- How long do animations last, and can players leave them early?

## After the campaign

### IDEA-001 — Standalone minigames with WhatsApp invitations

Date: 2026-11-07
Author: Pierre
Scope: After the campaign
Status: Exploring

After the birthday campaign, players can access each minigame independently. A player creates a lobby, and the game sends an invitation to a WhatsApp group with the minigame name and a join link. Others click the link, join the lobby and ready up. The creator starts the round when everyone is ready.

Send the invitation when the lobby opens so others have time to join. The WhatsApp integration mechanism remains to be decided.

Why:
Keep playing together after the birthday evening.

Open questions:
- Which minigames work well independently?
- Does the creator choose whether to notify the group?
- Can remote players play entirely from their phones?
- What happens when someone joins after a round has started?
